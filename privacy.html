Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

In short: this website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. We only receive the details you choose to send us through the contact form or while working with us, and we only use them to reply to you and manage your project.

1. Who we are

Nexasporte is the brand of an independent web designer based in Antwerp, Belgium. For anything related to your personal data, contact us at info@nexasporte.com.

2. What data we receive and why

When you use the contact form, your own email app opens with a pre-filled message. If you choose to send it, we receive:

  • your name, email address and (optionally) phone number;
  • the name of your club or academy, your sport and the plan you are interested in;
  • anything you write in your message.

We use this only to answer your question, prepare a proposal and, if we work together, to carry out the project. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in replying to messages (Article 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

We never sell your data, never use it for advertising and never share it with third parties, except the email provider mentioned below.

3. How long we keep it

  • Inquiries: if we don’t end up working together, we delete your messages within 12 months.
  • Active & paused plans: if you become a client, we keep your data while your plan is active. If you pause or freeze your Monthly Plan (for example during the off-season), we securely keep your website files, code and club data for up to 6 months so you can easily reactivate your site. If you don’t reactivate within those 6 months, we permanently delete everything.
  • Permanent cancellation: if you ask us to permanently cancel your account, we delete your website code and all related club data within 30 days of your request.
  • Legal records: we keep invoices and basic billing details for as long as Belgian accounting and tax law requires (currently 10 years).

4. Services we use

  • Email: messages are stored in a Proton Mail mailbox (Proton AG, Switzerland). Switzerland is recognised by the EU as offering an adequate level of data protection.
  • Hosting: this website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, it may log your IP address for security and to keep the site running. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Fonts and images are served from this website itself, so loading the page does not contact Google or other third parties.

5. Cookies

This website does not set any cookies and does not use analytics or tracking tools, so there is nothing to accept or refuse.

Links to other websites (for example our sponsored club, FC Vezhdanisht) open their site, which has its own privacy policy.

6. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, to limit how we use it, to object to its use, or to receive a copy of it. Just email info@nexasporte.com and we will reply within one month.

If you are not happy with how we handle your data, you can file a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
